Public Boat Landing Directory

A researched inventory of publicly managed or historically designated public boat-launch sites. Privately owned commercial marinas and paid private facilities are excluded. Listings are public-access records, not guarantees of free launching.

Important distinction: Unverified Public Launch means a public-access listing whose current fee or access arrangements have not been independently confirmed. Reported Free Public Launch identifies a user-reported no-fee ramp, not an independently verified fee policy. Closed means not currently available for launching. This directory is provided for reference, not as a promise of free access.

How this list was researched

This directory uses an older South Carolina public-ramp inventory supplemented by more recent county and Santee Cooper references. Public-access records are listed for convenience; the label Unverified Public Launch does not represent confirmation of current fees, availability or operating rules.
