ABOUT ALL THINGS SANTEE COOPER

Local knowledge. Independent research. Free information and premium resources.

All Things Santee Cooper (ATSC) is an independent information and publishing business focused on Lake Marion, Lake Moultrie, and the connected Santee Cooper waterways.

We serve the lake community, and we are also in business to earn revenue from the original work we create.

What stays free

We publish public-interest information including lake levels, wind forecasts, water conditions, boating considerations, public access resources, and general fishing education. Much of the underlying data is available directly from public agencies. We organize it, add context, and help readers understand what it may mean on the water.

Access to these public reports does not require a paid membership.

What we charge for

Original research and specialized resources take time, experience, equipment, travel, and publishing work. Some of that work is sold as premium content or services.

  • Detailed fishing and contour-map analysis, verified drift plans, and personalized guidance
  • Expanded historical research, curated map collections, books, guides, and other publications
  • Optional Patreon memberships with additional fishing information and benefits
  • Clearly identified advertising opportunities for approved businesses

Prices and what each purchase includes are presented with the relevant offering. You do not have to purchase anything to participate in our free community resources.

How we work

We combine firsthand time on the water with research, public records, mapping, and published sources. We distinguish observation from interpretation, identify uncertainty, and correct meaningful errors. Payment does not buy favorable editorial coverage or change the facts we report.

Read our mission and reporting standards for more detail.

For the Santee Cooper community

ATSC is for anglers, boaters, residents, visitors, history enthusiasts, and local businesses. We welcome useful conversations, firsthand experiences, photographs, and questions. Our goal is to make free information genuinely useful while offering premium products worth choosing—not pressuring members to buy.
